## Task 0.7 — Realistic outlook

Phase 1 alone is expected to reclaim ~1.4 s in `test_virtual_table.py`. Even applied to the slowest shard, that's ~1.1% of the gap to 90 s. The bulk of the runtime is in the integration tests (build_queue_screen ~45 s on its own across 44 tests — many of those over 1 s each), `race_setup_ships_smoke`, `quickstart_designs` parametrized tests, and the always-large `test_main_integration::test_game_instantiation` (~13 s alone). Phases 2 + 3 might collectively reclaim a few more seconds via fixture rescope, but Phase 4 (`strategy_screen` 50-test refactor) is almost certainly required to hit < 90 s. Phase 4 trigger is already armed.

## Pre-flight surprises to surface

1. **Python 3.11 baseline, not 3.13.** Project CLAUDE.md says Python 3.13+ baseline. Only 3.11.9 is installed. All tests pass under 3.11; deltas measured here are 3.11 deltas. The < 90 s target may be easier or harder under 3.13 — calibration unknown.
2. **Run 1 had a transient race_setup_screen collection error.** `tests/unit/ui/screens/test_race_setup_screen.py` failed to import in shard 6 with `ModuleNotFoundError: No module named 'tests.fixtures.race_setup_ui_builders'` — but the module exists, imports fine in isolation, and runs 2 + 3 collected and passed it cleanly. Likely an order-dependent shard-spawn race. Pre-existing flake; not introduced by anything in PROJ-327.
3. **PROJ-322 file path drift, mild.** `test_virtual_table.py` is at `tests/unit/ui/components/table/test_virtual_table.py` (under a new `table/` subdir), not `tests/unit/ui/components/test_virtual_table.py` as PROJ-322 cited. `test_ship_io.py` moved from `tests/unit/simulation/` to `tests/unit/ui/services/`. Both files have the original tests intact — deferrals are still valid, just with updated paths.
4. **Run 2 vs run 3 variance: 12 s.** The `±10%` noise in the design.md is real on this hardware — taking the median of 3 is the right call.
