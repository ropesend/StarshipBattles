| 2026-05-05 | Task 2.1 helper shape: a generator `iter_facility_ability_entries(facility, ability_name, registries)` yielding normalized dicts; multiplier-callback for `harvesting_engine.py:258` is **caller pre-applies** | The 8 candidate sites use the iterate→extract→read-field pattern in materially different ways: 3 sites sum a single numeric field across all entries (water/quality/atmosphere `_extract_X_modifier`), 2 sites filter to one comp_id then read a scalar with a typed default (planet_action_engine `_get_energy_drain_rate` / `_get_deactivation_time`), 1 site needs `resolve_size_multiplier(comp)` mid-iteration (harvesting_engine `_collect_storage_from_facility`). A scalar-only `get_ability_field_from_facility(facility, ability_name, field_name, default)` cannot serve all three shapes without contortion. The chosen primitive — a generator that yields `(comp, entry)` pairs where `entry` is always a dict (single dicts wrapped, lists exploded, scalars wrapped as `{"value": x}`) — collapses the layer/comp iteration + ability extraction + list/dict normalization that is the actual duplicated boilerplate, while letting each call site keep its own field arithmetic. The `comp` is yielded alongside the entry so callers needing `resolve_size_multiplier(comp)` (harvesting_engine) get it for free without a callback. |
