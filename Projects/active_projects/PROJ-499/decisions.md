Pinning the ability side as the existing curated projection means new ability attributes are silently invisible unless someone widens `snapshot_ability_stats()`. Both behaviors are intentional. Future project may either: (a) narrow the stats side to a curated projection, or (b) widen the ability side to raw; not this one. |
