MOAW

- hex grid that rolls over heading right eventually has a unit on the left side,
- grid can be approx 40x40 to 120x120
- there are 4 types of tiles on the grid: water, land, maintain, city
- There are 9 units:
   - Army
   - Paratroopers
   - Fighter
   - Bomber
   - Transport
   - Destroyer
   - Submarine
   - Carrier
   - Battleship
- Cities
   - scattered across the map in fixed locations
   - cannot be destroyed
   - start unclaimed
   - rolling a army into a city claims the city
   - once owned, cities provide production
   - cities can build one unit at a time
   - different units will have different times to be produced
- Units
	- All unit stats should be defined in a .json file, as little as possible should be hard coded
	- Army
		- moves on land only
		- cannot travel over mountains or water
		- moves at 1 hex per turn
		- unlimited range
		- 4 turns to build
		- can attack any other unit
	- Patatroopers
		- fly (moves over all terain)
		- moves 6 hexes per turn
		- range of 32 hexes before it needs to refule
		- When over land (Not maintains, not cities, not water) can be converted to a Army.  Army's cannot be converted to Paratroopers
		- 6 turns to build
		- cannot attack
	- fighter 
		- fly
		- moves 16 hexes per turn
		- range 16 hexes
		- 8 turns to build
		- can attack any other unit
	- Bomber
		- fly
		- moves 4 hexes per turn
		- range 32 hexes
		- 12 turns to build
		- When they attack, the bomber is destroyed and all units in the attacked hex, and the neighbor hexes are aliminated, any claimed cities in the blast zone become unclaimed
	    - Can attack armies, cities, submarines, battleships, destroyers, transports
	- Transport
		- Boat (can only move on water)
		- moves 4 hexes per turn
		- unlimited range
		- can carry up to 6 armies - arrmies are loaded by moving them onto the transport.
		- 2 turns to build
	- Destroyer
		- Boat
		- moves 6 hexes per turn
		- unlimited range
		- 6 turns to build
		- can attack any unit
	- Submarine
		- Boat
		- moves 4 hexes per turn
		- unlimited range
		- 6 turns to build
		- can mattack submarines, battleships, destroyers
	- Carrier
		- Boat
		- moves 4 hexes per turn
		- unlimited range
		- serves a landing/refuling station for aircraft
		- Cannot attack anything
		- 12 turns to build
	- Battleship
		- Boat
		- moves 4 hexes per turn
		- unlimited range
		- 12 turns to build
		- can attack all units
		
		
At game start:
select map size
select number of players
map is created - players are distributed so each has one city.  Each player should be on a separate land mass.
