1. **Completeness of removal** — `grep -rn -E "galaxy\._(global_hex|planet_to_system|zone_to_system)\b" .` should return only audit/tracking docs. Confirm no live caller remains.

2. **Migration target — is `_state.<field>` the right destination?** This is the most important review question.
