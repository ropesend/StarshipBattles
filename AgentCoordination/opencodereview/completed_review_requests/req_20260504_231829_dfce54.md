## What to look for

1. **Behavior accuracy** — pick 8-10 tests across the 4 projects and verify against production. UI tests are heavy on mocks — confirm assertions check meaningful properties (color tuples, positions, called-with-args), not just `mock.called == True`.

2. **Vacuous tests** — review-4 already replaced 10 vacuous tests across PROJ-338/339, but new ones may have crept in. Look for:
   - construction tests that assign attribute then read it back (tautology)
   - `is not None` checks on fields that production unconditionally sets
