## Instructions

PROJ-370 is the largest production refactor in the strategy chain — 4 mutator protocols, 4 write services, 26 write sites routed across ~10 production files. Focus areas in priority order:

1. **AST guard correctness and coverage.** Each of 4 guards must catch direct attribute writes from outside the allowlist while not over-matching legitimate access. Walk through each guard:
   - Read `tests/unit/strategy/data/_mutator_ast_walker.py` and the 4 guard activations in `test_mutator_boundary_ast_guard.py`. Confirm: does the walker correctly distinguish `obj.attr = value` from `self.attr = value` (the latter inside the data class itself, which should be allowed)?
   - The agent reported the walker was hardened with `self.X`/`cls.X` exclusion during Phase 2 because the original walker tripped on `WarpPoint.location` and intrinsic_abilities setup. Verify the exclusion is sound — does it still catch `obj.attr = value` where `obj` is a parameter or a local variable? It should.
   - Spot-check the allowlist for each guard. Is anything allowlisted that shouldn't be? Are the allowlisted files genuinely the data class itself + its write service + serialization paths?
   - Specifically: ShipInstance has 19 allowlisted paths — that's the largest. Is each one justified?

2. **Write-site routing soundness.** For each of the 26 routed write sites, confirm:
   - The original direct assignment was replaced with a mutator call.
   - The mutator call passes the same arguments / produces the same effect.
   - No write was missed (the AST guard catches misses, but spot-check 5-8 sites manually).
   - The `add_move_order_if_needed` change in `handlers/base.py:78-82` (now routes `fleet.path` through `session.fleet_mutator.set_path`) — confirm it's semantically identical to the prior direct assignment.

3. **GameSession wiring + TurnEngineConfig integration.** Per the joint review, mutators are constructed at `game/strategy/engine/game_session.py:99-108` and threaded through `TurnEngineConfig.create_default()`. Verify:
   - Read `game_session.py` and confirm: are all 4 mutators constructed exactly once per session?
   - Read `turn_engine_config.py` and confirm the 4 new mutator fields are properly wired through `create_default()`.
   - Confirm: NO references to `_facade_state.py` for mutator wiring (the original Wave A draft had this wrong; joint review caught it).
   - Are the lazy-defaults in services (e.g. `_get_empire_mutator()` in `superweapon_order_processor.py:70-76`, `_get_ship_mutator()` in `transfer_branches.py:173`) safe? Each is a fallback for when the mutator isn't threaded; do they construct equivalently to the GameSession path?

4. **Empire `prune_empty_fleets` lift soundness.** The post-battle empty-fleet pruning was previously inline at `combat/post_battle_hook.py:200-218` and is now `EmpireWriteService.prune_empty_fleets`. Verify:
   - The lifted method behaves identically to the inline version (same iteration order, same removal semantics).
   - The hook signature change (now accepts `empire_mutator` kwarg) doesn't break existing callers. The agent claimed lazy-defaults preserve backward compat — verify.

5. **ShipInstance Q1 — manager forwarding.** Per the joint review, `IShipInstanceMutator` covers `cargo_contents`/`consumable_levels` writes via 1-line forwarding from `ShipCargoManager`/`ShipConsumableManager`. Verify:
   - The forwarding is in place (mutator method calls into the manager).
   - The manager is in the AST guard's allowlist OR is wrapped by the mutator (so manager-internal writes also flow through the guard).
   - The agent's choice (forwarding seam vs allowlisting managers) matches the joint review resolution.

6. **TurnEngineConfig field count growth.** Config grew from 18 fields to 22 — 4 new mutator fields. The agent flagged that tests using `dataclasses.replace(cfg, ...)` on a default-instantiated `TurnEngineConfig()` will see 4 None mutator fields. Verify:

7. **Cross-cutting.** Look for: layering violations (mutators reaching into UI?), broad `except Exception` without rationale, missing return-type annotations, files approaching 500 LOC.
