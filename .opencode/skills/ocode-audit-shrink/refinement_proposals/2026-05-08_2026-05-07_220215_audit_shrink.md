## 1. False-Positive Patterns

### FP-1: Visually-similar render functions with parameterized magic numbers

- **Examples (rejected findings):**
  - `DUP-X-04` — `game/ui/effects/hit_effects.py:146-219` — rejected because the three render functions (`_draw_armor_hit`, `_draw_component_destroyed`, `_draw_ship_destroyed`) share a structural skeleton but diverge in concrete tuning parameters: 6 vs 8 radiating lines, stroke widths `int(2 * (1 - t))` vs `int(3 * (1 - t))`, line-length factors 1.3 vs 1.4, and `_draw_ship_destroyed` includes a unique conditional flash effect (lines 222-226) absent from the others. These are deliberate visual specializations, not copy-paste duplication.
- **Proposed filter:**
  - **Phase 1 deterministic** (preferred): in `Tools/audit_shrink/clones.py` (or wherever the clone-detector post-processing lives), downrate any cluster whose member functions match a render-loop heuristic — namely, two or more numeric literal differences AND a difference in conditional-block count between any pair of cluster members. Render functions tend to encode their "personality" entirely in literals; consolidating them costs more in config-dict noise than it saves in LOC.
  - **Phase 2 prompt guidance:** add to the duplication-hunter agent's "What NOT to Report" section: *"Render / draw functions that share a structural shape but differ in numeric tuning parameters (line counts, stroke widths, multipliers) or in optional conditional effects should be classified MINOR/INFO, not MAJOR. Parameterizing them tends to replace clear specialized code with a config-dict lookup at no real LOC saving."*
- **Risk of filtering:** Could suppress legitimately consolidatable render code (e.g. a true 100% byte-equivalent helper). Mitigation: only apply the downrate when ≥ 2 numeric-literal divergences are present.
- **Recommendation:** ADD_TO_PROMPT (Phase 2 is the cheap path; Phase 1 deterministic is a stretch goal if the heuristic proves stable).
